QUESTION:
How could a council explore digital exclusion among older residents?

----------------------------------------------------------------------

RESEARCH BRIEF:

## Problem
Digital exclusion refers to the lack of access to digital technologies, such as computers, smartphones, and the internet, which can hinder older adults' ability to participate in modern society. This can lead to social isolation, reduced access to essential services, and decreased economic opportunities.

## Research Questions
1. What are the prevalence and characteristics of digital exclusion among older residents in our local area?
2. How do socio-demographic factors (e.g., age, income, education) influence digital inclusion or exclusion among older adults?
3. Are there existing community resources or initiatives that address digital exclusion among older adults, and what is their effectiveness?

## Population or Groups
The population of interest are older residents aged 65+ living in the local authority's area, with a focus on those who may be more likely to experience digital exclusion due to socioeconomic factors.

## Potentially useful data or variables
- Household income and expenditure data
- Age-related health outcomes (e.g., isolation rates, mental health)
- Participation in community activities, including those related to technology use
- Public transport accessibility and urban planning information
- Digital literacy and skills training programs available locally

## Suggestions
1. Conduct a survey or focus groups among older residents to assess their digital access and usage habits.
2. Analyze existing data from the local council's own records (e.g., electoral rolls, benefits claims) for demographic information on residents who may be more likely to experience digital exclusion.
3. Review existing community programs that target seniors and evaluate their effectiveness in promoting digital inclusion.

## Explain how the findings may support a decision
The research could inform policy decisions regarding the development of targeted initiatives to address digital exclusion among older adults, such as increased public transport options or community centers offering free digital skills training. Understanding the prevalence and causes of digital exclusion can also help identify areas where existing services might need improvement.

## State assumptions and missing information
Assumptions include that digital exclusion among older residents is a significant concern in our local area and that there is no comprehensive dataset available to assess this issue directly. Missing information could include current data on the demographic characteristics of residents who are digitally excluded and the effectiveness of existing community programs aimed at addressing this issue.
