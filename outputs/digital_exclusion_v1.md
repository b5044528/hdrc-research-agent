QUESTION:
How could a council explore digital exclusion among older residents?

----------------------------------------------------------------------

RESEARCH BRIEF:

## Problem
The issue of digital exclusion among older residents is a pressing concern for local authorities. This problem can lead to social isolation, reduced access to essential services, and decreased quality of life for vulnerable populations.

## Research Questions
1. What proportion of older adults in the local area experience difficulties accessing digital technologies (e.g., computers, smartphones, internet)?
2. How do existing barriers (e.g., cost, lack of digital literacy, accessibility issues) contribute to digital exclusion among this population?
3. Can targeted interventions or initiatives effectively reduce digital exclusion and improve digital inclusion for older adults?

## Population or Groups
The target population for this research is older adults aged 65+ living in the local area, with a focus on those who may be at higher risk of digital exclusion due to factors such as low income, limited mobility, or cognitive impairment.

## Potentially useful data or variables
- Demographic data (age, income level, education level)
- Household budget and expenditure patterns
- Digital literacy levels and skills gaps
- Access to healthcare services and support networks
- Online participation rates and digital habits

## Suggestions
1. Conduct a survey using existing datasets (e.g., census, social services records) to gather information on the prevalence of digital exclusion among older adults.
2. Analyze online behavior data from local libraries, community centers, or other public institutions providing digital access to identify patterns and barriers.
3. Utilize existing research studies or literature reviews focusing on digital exclusion, social isolation, and aging.

## Explain how the findings may support a decision
The study's results will inform the development of targeted interventions aimed at reducing digital exclusion among older adults. By understanding the scope of the issue, identifying key barriers, and exploring effective solutions, policymakers can make informed decisions about resource allocation and program development to address this social problem.

## State assumptions and missing information
Assumptions:
- The local authority has access to relevant datasets and research studies on digital exclusion and aging.
- Existing services and support networks are adequate in addressing the needs of older adults.

Missing Information:
- A detailed understanding of the specific context and challenges faced by the target population.
- Data on the cost-effectiveness and feasibility of proposed interventions.
