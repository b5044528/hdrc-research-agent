QUESTION:
How could a council investigate inequalities in fuel poverty?

----------------------------------------------------------------------

RESEARCH BRIEF:

## Problem

Inequalities in fuel poverty refer to disparities in the level of warmth and comfort experienced by individuals and households, particularly those from low-income backgrounds. This issue is often exacerbated by factors such as housing conditions, income levels, and access to energy resources.

## Research Questions

1. What are the current prevalence rates of fuel poverty across different socio-economic groups within the local authority area?
2. Is there a correlation between fuel poverty and other social determinants, such as housing quality, education level, or employment status?
3. How effective are existing policies and interventions in addressing fuel poverty, and what gaps exist in current support services?

## Population or Groups

The population of interest includes:

* All residents within the local authority area
* Specific socio-economic groups (e.g., low-income households, pensioners)
* Households with varying levels of housing conditions (e.g., energy-efficient homes, those in need of repair)

## Potentially useful data or variables

Potentially useful data could include:
* Energy consumption patterns and prices
* Housing characteristics (e.g., insulation quality, heating type)
* Household income and expenditure data
* Demographic information (e.g., age, disability status)
* Existing policies and interventions related to fuel poverty support services

## Suggestions

1. Analyze existing data on energy consumption, housing characteristics, and household income through the local authority's administrative records.
2. Conduct surveys or interviews with a representative sample of households to gather additional information on fuel poverty experiences and perceptions.
3. Review existing policies and reports from government agencies, non-profit organizations, and other stakeholders to identify effective strategies and gaps in support services.

## Explain how the findings may support a decision

The findings will inform the development of targeted interventions and policy recommendations to address fuel poverty disparities within the local authority area. By understanding the scope and nature of the issue, policymakers can make informed decisions about resource allocation, service design, and advocacy efforts to promote energy efficiency and access to affordable heating.

## State assumptions and missing information

Assumptions:

* The data available is representative of the population of interest.
* There are no existing datasets specifically focused on fuel poverty within the local authority area.

Missing information:

* Details about specific socio-economic groups experiencing higher levels of fuel poverty.
* Quantitative data on the impact of existing policies and interventions on fuel poverty outcomes.
